Why landlords use it

The register book, the calculator and the phone reminders — replaced.

Six things that normally live in three notebooks and someone's memory.

🏢

Buildings and flats

Add a building once and tell it "10 floors, 3 flats each" — the whole plan is created for you, each flat with its own rent and advance.

🔑

Tenants and agreements

Who moved in, how much advance they paid, how many months the agreement runs and when it ends. You get a warning before it expires.

৳

Rent collection

Create the whole month's rent list in one click, then mark who paid in full, who paid part and who is still due.

🧾

Bills and repairs

Electricity, water, gas, garbage, lift servicing, wiring, plumbing — recorded by category so you can see where the money goes.

Staff salary

Profit, per building

Owner or manager

Hand the daily work over without handing over your accounts.

Some owners look after their buildings themselves. Others keep a manager who collects rent, pays the electricity bill and calls the plumber. Baribari supports both.

  • Create a separate login for your manager in seconds
  • Decide which buildings he is responsible for
  • He can collect rent and record bills, but cannot delete buildings or hire other managers
  • Every rent entry records who collected it

How it works

Set up in an evening, useful from the first month.

Add your buildings

Name, address and how many floors. Let it build the floors and flats for you, then fix the rent for any flat that is different.

Put your tenants in

Add each tenant with their NID and phone, then place them in a flat with the agreement dates and the advance you took.

Work month by month

Generate the rent list, record payments and bills as they happen, and read the profit at the end of the month.

Month end

"Did this building make money in September?"

The report answers it in one line, and shows the working underneath: every flat's rent, every bill with its vendor, every salary paid. Change the month from the dropdown and the whole page follows.

  • Income, dues, expenses, salaries and net profit side by side
  • Six-month income against spending chart on the dashboard
  • Print or save as PDF straight from the browser
