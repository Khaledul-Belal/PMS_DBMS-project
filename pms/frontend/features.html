Features

Everything a building needs, in one account

Baribari covers the whole cycle: the building itself, the people renting it, the money coming in and the money going out.

🏢

Building and floor plan

Every building holds floors, every floor holds flats, shops, offices or garages. The floor plan view colours each unit green, amber or red so you can see the building at a glance.

⚡

Bulk setup

Ten floors with three flats each is one form and one click. Then edit any single flat that has a different rent, size or number of bedrooms.

🔑

Tenant records

Name, phone, NID, occupation, family size, permanent address and an emergency contact - everything you would otherwise keep on a photocopy in a drawer.

📄

Agreements

Start date, duration, monthly rent, advance taken and the rent due day. Agreements ending within sixty days are flagged so you can start looking for a tenant early.

৳

Rent collection

One button creates the rent line for every active agreement. Record full, partial or missed payments with the method - cash, bKash, Nagad, bank or cheque.

🧾

Bills by category

Electricity, water, gas, garbage, lift, wiring, plumbing, painting, security. Each bill can belong to the whole building or to one flat.

🔧

Maintenance jobs

Log what broke, how urgent it is and who is fixing it. Marking a job done with a cost writes the expense for you, so no repair is ever missed in the accounts.

👷

Staff and salary

Caretakers, guards and cleaners attached to a building, with a monthly salary sheet you can generate and tick off as you pay.

📊

Reports

Per building, per month: rent collected, rent unpaid, bills, salaries and net profit - plus a six-month chart of income against spending.

Three roles

Everyone sees exactly what they should.

The system separates the person who owns the buildings, the person who runs them day to day and the person who runs the platform.

  • Owner - adds buildings, hires managers, sees every report
  • Manager - collects rent, records bills, calls the plumber, pays the staff
  • Admin - looks after subscribers, plans and the platform itself
  • One owner's data can never be seen by another owner - every query is scoped by account
Under the hood

Built the way the course asked for it.

16 related tables

Owners, managers, properties, floors, units, tenants, leases, rent payments, expenses, categories, staff, salaries, maintenance, plans, subscriptions and admin.

Normalised to 3NF

No repeating groups, no partial dependency, no transitive dependency. Categories and plans live in their own tables rather than as repeated text.

Safe by default

Bcrypt password hashing, prepared statements for every query, session checks on every endpoint and escaped output in the browser.
