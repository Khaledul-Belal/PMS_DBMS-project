FAQ

Questions people ask us

If your question is not here, the contact page reaches us directly.

Using the system

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. If you can use a phone you can use this. Every screen is a list with an Add button, and nothing is deleted without asking you first.

Can my manager use it instead of me?

Yes. Create a manager from your dashboard and give him the email and password. He sees the same buildings but cannot delete them or hire other managers.

What if one flat has a different rent from the others?

Build the floors in bulk, then open any flat and change its rent, size or number of bedrooms.

A tenant paid only half the rent. Can I record that?

Yes. Enter the amount you actually received and the line is marked partial, with the remainder showing in the month's dues.

A tenant is leaving. What do I do?

End the agreement from the Leases page. The flat immediately becomes vacant and is ready for the next tenant, while the old agreement stays in the history.

Does the repair cost go into my accounts?

When you mark a maintenance job as done and type the cost, an expense is created for that building automatically, so the profit figure is always honest.

Account and data

Can another landlord see my buildings?

No. Every request checks who is logged in and filters by account, so one owner's data is invisible to every other owner.

Are my passwords safe?

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. Even with a copy of the database, nobody can read them.

What happens if the admin deletes my account?

Everything that belongs to it goes with it - buildings, tenants, agreements, bills and managers. The database does this with cascade deletes, so nothing is left orphaned.

For the examiner

Where is the ER diagram?

In the docs folder. Open er-diagram.html in a browser for the Chen notation diagram with relationship diamonds, and a second page with every entity's attributes.

Is the schema normalised?

Yes, to third normal form. The reasoning, including the one deliberate exception, is written out in docs/ER-DIAGRAM.md.

How do I run it?

Copy the folder into XAMPP's htdocs, import database/schema.sql through phpMyAdmin and open http://localhost/pms/frontend/.
