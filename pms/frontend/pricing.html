Questions about billing

How do I pay?

In this student version the subscription is recorded with a transaction id when you sign up. A real bKash or Nagad gateway would slot into the same table without any schema change.

What happens when my plan runs out?

The subscription is marked expired. Your data stays exactly where it is - you simply renew to keep adding buildings.

Can I change plan later?

Yes. Each purchase is a new row in the subscription table, so your whole payment history is kept.

What if I have more buildings than my plan allows?

The system stops you from adding one more and tells you which plan to move to. Nothing is deleted.
